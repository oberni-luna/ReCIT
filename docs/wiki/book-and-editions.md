# Fiche livre, œuvres et éditions

One book screen over Work, Edition and InventoryItem. Editions are inventaire.io (or Wikidata) entities: the app shows them, creates the missing ones, edits every claim, spots duplicates and merges them.

## Code

- `Features/Book/` — `BookDetailView`, `BookViewModel`, `BookAnchor`
- `Features/EditEdition/`, `Features/CreateBook/`
- `AppModels/Entity/` — `EntityModel`, `WorkEditionResolver`
- `Model/Books/` — `Work`, `Edition`, `Author`, `EditionRelevance`

## Features

- [0020 Créer un livre qu'inventaire.io n'a pas](features/0020-creating-a-book-that-inventaire-lacks.md) — un scan sans résultat devient une contribution : titre, auteur, couverture photographiée, et deux écrans qui empêchent le doublon d'auteur et d'œuvre
- [0029 A book's cover, full screen](features/0029-full-screen-cover.md) — tap the header's cover to see it fitted to the whole screen; double-tap between fit and fill, pinch, drag, swipe down to close
- [0030 Works, authors and editions upserted in place](features/0030-entities-upserted-in-place.md) — one way into the store for entities, so a sync stops wiping genres and colours; what removed issue 0067's row; writes after a round trip re-read or skip what has gone
- [0047 Nouveau livre, l'œuvre d'abord](features/0047-new-book-work-first.md) — a scan inventaire.io does not know opens on a search for the work: a work found leaves only the edition to describe, none found creates the work with its authors and the edition in one form; covers from the camera, Photos, Files or an address; a notice and a « ? » about bibliographic data; a known work now named in `resolve`, an uploaded cover sent absolute; seen on the simulator up to « Nouvelle œuvre », never published, e2e not run
- [0049 Modifier une édition, depuis la fiche livre](features/0049-edit-an-edition.md) — « Données bibliographiques › Modifier » at the foot of the book screen, for every edition but a Wikidata one: cover, title, subtitle, the work (picked through `V2`'s search or created) and authors added to it, read fresh from `by-uris`, one `update-claim` per claim that moved, not optimistic, a retry sends only what has not landed; seen on the simulator, never saved, e2e not run
- [0050 Toutes les données bibliographiques d'une édition, de la fiche livre et du scanner](features/0050-every-bibliographic-datum.md) — the book screen ends on one tinted « Données bibliographiques » row; « Modifier l'édition » shows and edits every property inventaire.io accepts on an edition (publisher, collection, date, language, format, counts, six contributor roles, 22 catalogue ids, ten links; ISBN read-only, « Différente de » not done), lists its works — added, detached with a swipe, changed in « Modifier l'œuvre », the only place authors are added —, pins « Enregistrer »; the scanner's « Nouvelle édition » is the same form through `resolve`; never saved for real, e2e not run
- [0051 Doublons : l'écran « Éditions » repère les doublons, et les fusionne sur inventaire.io](features/0051-edition-duplicates.md) — each edition row shows « éditeur · collection · année » and its ISBN or « Sans ISBN »; the screen tags « Même ISBN » / « Doublon probable » / « Doublon signalé » (open merge tasks) and heads the list with a banner; « Les comparer » reviews one pair at a time — the one kept checked (Wikidata always), « Ce qui diffère », « Fusionner » server-first through `PUT /api/entities/merge` (merged or handed to inventaire.io's team), « Ce ne sont pas les mêmes » as an optimistic `wdt:P1889`; no merge ever made for real, never seen on a simulator, e2e not run
- [0054 Forms on the book screen's dark cards, through `background/shade`](features/0054-forms-on-the-book-screens-dark-cards.md) — in dark, a form's cards sit a step below a `gray/800` ground, a veil of black at 40 % (`.backgroundShade`, Figma's new `background/shade`) instead of grey on black; the book screen reads the same two tokens; light unchanged; « Modifier l'édition » and a swipe on a card unseen, e2e not run
- [0057 Modifier une œuvre et un·e auteur·ice, chacun avec son « Enregistrer »](features/0057-edit-works-and-authors.md) — an inventaire.io work of « Édition de » opens the whole of « Modifier l'œuvre » (type, form, original language, date, authors and every role, series and rank, genres and subjects, relations, 20 catalogues, links, « À ne pas confondre avec »), an inventaire.io person « Modifier l'auteur·ice » (names, pseudonyms, languages, dates, movement, influences, 29 catalogues, links); each saved alone, not optimistic, « Quitter sans enregistrer ? » on the way back; `EditionProperty.Catalogue` per entity type, `EntityEdit`; nothing saved for real, e2e not run
- [0058 « Éditions » par langue, et une œuvre de 7 742 éditions qui ne gèle plus](features/0058-editions-language-filter.md) — the language shown at the right of « Éditions de … », a menu of the languages by number of editions, the phone's by default, « Langue non renseignée » for editions without `wdt:P407`; the banner and « Les comparer » follow it; the book screen stops inserting every edition, the duplicate detection stops comparing every pair, editions held are refetched once a day; seen on the simulator in dark only, e2e not run
- [0059 A spinner after « Œuvres de … » and « Éditions de … » while they are fetched](features/0059-section-loading-indicator.md) — the author and the « Éditions » screens show the cache, then a small grey `SectionLoadingIndicator` after the section header's label for as long as `load` runs; one spinner at a time on an empty « Éditions »; seen on the simulator in dark only, e2e not run
- [0063 Names, titles and descriptions in the phone's language](features/0063-content-in-the-phones-language.md) — every `lang=fr` becomes `ContentLanguage.preferred`, read from `Locale.preferredLanguages`; `EntityLabel` and the new `EntityDescription` put it first; the store is not migrated, an entity turns on its next fetch; Android still in French; not seen on a simulator, e2e not run
- [0070 Linking a Wikidata account, and editing Wikidata works and authors](features/0070-wikidata-account-and-editing.md) — « Compte et connexion » ends on « Wikidata »: link through `GET /api/auth/wikidata-oauth` in a `WKWebView` handed the session, `enabledOAuth` says it worked; Wikidata works and people open the whole « Modifier l'œuvre » / « Modifier l'auteur·ice », which inventaire.io sends on to Wikidata under the reader's account or its bot; their pickers name Wikidata entities only; never linked or saved for real, not seen on a simulator, e2e not run
- [0079 The lists a book is in, on the Fiche livre](features/0079-the-lists-a-book-is-in.md) — « Dans les listes » between « Dans mon inventaire » and « Données bibliographiques »: my lists and my friends' lists of works holding the book, mixed, most recently updated first, absent when none; a tap opens the Liste, which springs from its first book to this one and rings its cover with a fading tinted halo, one haptic, VoiceOver on the title, Reduce Motion opens on it; installed on an iPhone, e2e not run
- [0080 A private note on my copy of a book](features/0080-a-private-note-on-my-copy.md) — « Ce que je garde pour moi » under « Ce que j'en pense » in « Dans mon inventaire »: the item's `notes`, which only its owner receives, saved like the Commentaire (1 s, focus loss, leaving), optimistic, reverted with a SnackBar; writes of one field on one book now leave one after the other; in English the Commentaire reads « What I think »; installed on an iPhone, e2e not run
